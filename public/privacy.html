ProSolver Hospitals: privacy information

Prelaunch draft, not a complete privacy notice. Do not activate data collection or invite the public until the operator has provided legally appropriate privacy disclosures and a valid contact method.

Intended data

Problems you type or record, daily work challenges, email, phone, consent timestamp, verification status, and referral relationships.

Intended uses

Reviewing requests, verifying email, operating a waitlist, administering points and referrals, and analyzing aggregated or appropriately de-identified problem themes to guide future services.

Intended processors

Cloudflare for API/database/private audio storage, and Resend for verification emails. GitHub Pages serves the public website.

Restrictions

Never submit patient names, identifying clinical details, health records, passwords, or confidential employer materials. Not an emergency service or medical decision-support service.

Before launch

The operator must add accurate data-controller identity and contact, lawful basis, retention periods, international transfer disclosures, access/deletion rights and the applicable Ghana data-protection requirements.
