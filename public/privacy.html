ProSolver Hospitals privacy information

Prelaunch draft. REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH: The operator must complete and approve this notice before collecting personal information. Registration is disabled.

Who is responsible

Data controller: REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH: legal operator identity, registration and address. Privacy contact: REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH: monitored email and postal contact.

What we intend to collect

Email address, international phone number, typed problem, optional daily challenges, optional voice note, referral relationship, verification and consent records, points history, and founder review notes. Phone numbers are not verified. Do not provide patient names, records, identifying clinical details, passwords, confidential hospital documents or sensitive employer information.

Why we intend to use it

To receive and review requests, operate the waitlist, contact you about your submission, verify email, prevent abuse, and administer referrals and submission credits. We may use aggregated or appropriately de-identified problem patterns for product research. We do not transfer identifiable information to Trivsel. Any additional marketing, model training, or identifiable sharing requires a separate assessment and permission where necessary.

Lawful basis and choices

REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH: identify and explain the applicable lawful basis for each purpose under the law that applies to the operator and participants. The form requires consent to contact about the submission and to this notice. Optional research permissions and any withdrawal process must be specified before expanding research use.

Processors and transfers

GitHub Pages serves the public site. Cloudflare Workers, D1 and private R2 process submissions and storage. Resend delivers verification emails. REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH: document actual processing locations, subprocessors, cross-border transfers, contracts and safeguards.

Retention and security

The planned application retention setting is 90 days, with expired unverified requests cleared after expiry. Private audio is stored in an R2 bucket without public access. Access is restricted to the founder dashboard or the submitting member's deletion workflow. REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH: approve actual retention periods, backup retention and recovery procedures, and explain residual risks.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion through the privacy contact above. Verified members can request deletion while signed in; the operator can delete individual submissions. REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH: explain the full applicable rights, response process, identity verification and complaint route. Ghana's Data Protection Commission may be relevant; confirm the applicable process.

Important limits

ProSolver Hospitals is not an emergency service or clinical decision support service. A point permits one extra verified problem submission; it has no cash value and does not guarantee human resolution.
